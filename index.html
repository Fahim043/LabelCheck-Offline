Safe edits: Only the corresponding label .txt is rewritten after Save. Before first modification, its original contents are backed up under labels/.labelcheck-backups/. Unsupported rows remain untouched.

Supported: YOLO detection (5 fields), YOLO segmentation polygons (7+ odd fields), and custom single point rows (3 fields). Pose/keypoint, non-YOLO coordinates and masks are not supported in this version.
